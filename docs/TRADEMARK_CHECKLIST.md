# C.P.S. — Trademark and Branding Readiness Checklist

## 1. Legal Notice & Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Rebranding the project to **C.P.S.** is a software engineering rebranding operation. Adopting a project name or adding copyright notices does **NOT** constitute a legally registered trademark or guarantee freedom to operate.

---

## 2. Branding Guidelines

- **Product Name:** `C.P.S.`
- **Full Display Name:** `C.P.S. — AI Coding Companion`
- **Product Category:** Multi-language AI coding companion, browser extension, and application security platform.
- **Symbol Notice:** Do **NOT** use the registered trademark symbol `®`. The unregistered trademark symbol `™` may be used informally (`C.P.S.™`) once legal clearance is underway.

---
